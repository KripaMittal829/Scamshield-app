Safety Awareness

Know the red flags. Protect yourself and your community.

🔑 Golden Rules
🚫

Never Share OTP, PIN, or CVV

No legitimate bank, company, or government body will ever ask for these over call or message.

🔗

Avoid Unknown Links

Don't click links in unsolicited SMS or WhatsApp messages. Go directly to official websites.

✅

Verify Before Transferring Money

Always call the person on a known number before sending money, even for urgent requests.

🏦

Banks Never Ask for Passwords

Legitimate banks never request passwords, OTPs, or account details through calls or SMS.

💸 UPI & Payment Fraud

• Approving a UPI collect request sends money — do NOT approve to "receive".

• Scammers send fake QR codes that deduct money instead of depositing.

• Urgent "last chance" payment links are almost always scams.

🏦 Bank Impersonation

• Account blocked/suspended messages → log in directly on the official app.

• KYC updates are done on official bank portals only, never via SMS links.

• Real bank officers never call asking for OTP or password.

💼 Fake Job Offers

• Legitimate employers never ask for registration fees upfront.

• Unrealistic salaries (₹50,000/month for 2 hours daily) are red flags.

• Verify companies on LinkedIn or their official website.

🧠 Social Engineering

• Creating emotional urgency (family emergency, police case) is manipulation.

• "Don't tell anyone" — a major red flag. Legitimate requests are transparent.

• Pause and think before acting on any fear/panic-inducing message.
